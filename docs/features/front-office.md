# Front Office System

---

# Purpose

Supports operational hotel activities performed by Front Office staff.

---

# Features

- Walk-in booking
- Manual booking
- Room assignment
- Guest check-in
- Guest check-out
- Payment handling
- Availability checking

---

# Check-in Rules

- Booking must be valid
- Room must be available
- Physical room may be assigned during check-in

---

# Check-out Rules

- Outstanding balance should be validated
- Room status should be updated after checkout

---

# Operational Notes

Front Office staff are scoped to their assigned hotel.
